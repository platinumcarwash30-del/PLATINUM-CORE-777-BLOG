PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy —
CORE REVIEW.

This Privacy Policy explains how CORE REVIEW, the mobile application connected to PLATINUM CORE 777, handles information when people create an account, use the test version or contact the project.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Back to Test Phase ↗ Ask a privacy question

1. Who is responsible?

CORE REVIEW and PLATINUM CORE 777 are an independent project created and operated by Marko Ćuća from Belgrade, Serbia. For privacy questions, requests or complaints about the handling of personal data, contact markoplatinum@icloud.com.

2. What information may be processed?

  • Account information: email address and authentication information needed to create and secure an account.
  • Profile information: name, surname, optional phone number and other details a user chooses to provide.
  • Role and approval information: information needed to connect a user with an approved company, owner or permitted role.
  • Service activity: verified visit information, reviews, ratings, notes, messages and notifications created through the service.
  • Technical and security information: limited logs and connection information needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.
  • Support communication: information included when a person contacts the project by email about testing, support, sponsorship, partnership or deletion.

CORE REVIEW is not designed to collect more information than is necessary for its controlled business and review functions. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details or government identification numbers in reviews, notes or messages.

3. Why is information used?

  • To create, verify, approve and secure user accounts.
  • To provide profile, company, role, verified-visit, review and notification features.
  • To connect authorized users with the correct company or owner context.
  • To detect abuse, protect the service and investigate technical problems.
  • To communicate with a user about support, testing, deletion or a question they sent.
  • To improve the test version and prepare more reliable releases.

4. Who may receive information?

Information may be shared only where needed to provide the service: with authorized owners, companies or roles connected to the relevant review context; with infrastructure and service providers that help provide authentication, hosting, database, email or security services; or when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and the project.

Personal information is not sold. CORE REVIEW is not intended to use user data for advertising personalization.

5. How is information protected?

Access is controlled by account, role and purpose. Data is transmitted through secure connections where supported by the service infrastructure, and reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to reduce unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid placing secrets or unnecessary sensitive information in the app.

6. Retention and account deletion

Information is kept only for as long as needed to provide the service, complete the test phase, protect the system, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. When a user requests account deletion, the project will process the request and delete or anonymize associated account data that is not required to be retained for a legitimate legal or security reason. Any necessary retention will be limited and explained when appropriate.

Deletion can be requested inside the app where the option is available or externally by emailing markoplatinum@icloud.com with the subject “CORE REVIEW Account Deletion”. Include the account email so the request can be identified. Do not send a password or verification code by email.

7. Children

CORE REVIEW is a controlled business and review application and is not directed to children. The project does not knowingly request personal information from children for independent account creation.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app, data practices or legal requirements change. The current version will remain publicly available on this page, with the update date shown above. Material changes will be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel where required.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, access requests, correction requests or account deletion: markoplatinum@icloud.com.
