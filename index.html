01THE CENTRAL IDEA

CORE REVIEW

The human connection inside the core.

PLATINUM CORE 777 is designed to connect people, clients, companies and licensed workspaces in one controlled and trusted environment.

CORE REVIEW is the control and orchestration module that connects roles, cases, documents, messages and business activity into a verifiable review flow for one case.

It is not an open social network. Connections are limited by roles, permissions and purpose. The right people can be connected around the right case without exposing everything to everyone.

The goal is not to connect everyone. The goal is to connect the right people, in the right context, with the right level of trust.

Author, Founder & CEO of PLATINUM CORE 777 & CORE REVIEW

PLATINUM CORE 777 is an independent project created by one person who publicly stands behind the idea, the development process and the decisions made along the way.

Public project documents, releases and statements are signed with the author's verified electronic identity key whenever applicable. The purpose is simple: people should be able to see who stands behind the work.
